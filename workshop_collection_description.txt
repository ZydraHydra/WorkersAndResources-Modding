[h1]Wait - What is a DLL Mod? (Step-by-Step Setup)[/h1]
Ordinary Steam Workshop mods only add buildings or vehicles. Because these mods add in-game [b]F8 / F9[/b] overlays and hook into game logic, they require a [b]Mod Loader[/b] alongside the game. Clicking Play in Steam alone will [b]not[/b] load DLL mods.

Both [b]TesmioLoader (v0.4.5+)[/b] and [b]Republic Mod Loader (v1.0.1)[/b] automatically scan your subscribed Steam Workshop mods - [b]no manual file copying required[/b]:

[b]Option A - TesmioLoader (Recommended - Mature & Updated for WRSR 1.2.0.9):[/b]
[list=1]
[*] Click [b]Subscribe to all[/b] on this collection.
[*] Download & extract [url=https://github.com/MaxLegend/TesmioLoader/releases]TesmioLoader (v0.4.5+)[/url] into your WRSR game directory ([url=https://maxlegend.github.io/TesmioLoaderDocs/#/getting-started]Docs[/url]).
[*] Run [b]tesmiolauncher.exe[/b], tick the mods you want, launch WRSR, and press [b]F9[/b] (or [b]F8[/b] for TradePriceLimits) in-game!
[/list]
[b]Option B - Republic Mod Loader (RML - All-on-Steam Alternative):[/b]
[list=1]
[*] Click [b]Subscribe to all[/b] on this collection.
[*] Also subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3787969749]Republic Mod Loader (RML)[/url] ([url=https://github.com/Ultimate-Universe/WRSR-RepublicModLoader]GitHub[/url]).
[*] Run [b]RepublicModLoader.exe[/b] from:
[code]<Your Steam Library>\steamapps\workshop\content\784150\3787969749\RepublicModLoader.exe[/code]
[*] Tick the mods you want, click [b]Launch WRSR[/b], and press [b]F9[/b] (or [b]F8[/b]) in-game!
[/list]
